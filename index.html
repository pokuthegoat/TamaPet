FAQ

Questions, answered.

A Tamagotchi-style game: adopt a pet, raise it and level it up — and every level you progress with your pet saves a real animal in real life.

A dog, a cat or a polar bear — and you give it a name.

Earn XP: feed and bathe your pet, play the minigames, finish your daily quests and spin the daily wheel. Every level you reach saves a real animal.

10 retro minigames, right on your pet’s screen: Snake, Pong, Bricks, Flap, Jump, Space, Blocks, Memory, Racer and Catch.

Five new quests every day — like beating a high score or playing three different games. Each one gives XP and TamaPoints.

Spend them in the shop on new colours for your device and your pet.
