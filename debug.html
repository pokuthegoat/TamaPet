How it would work

  • Pick an animal (♥ to flip through, ● to choose).
  • Home: level + XP bar at the top, your pet in the middle, 4 icons at the bottom — Feed, Bath, Play, Stats. ♥ moves the highlight, ● opens it.
  • Feed / Bath: a short animation, then XP.
  • Play: pick a retro minigame (Snake, Pong, Bricks) played right on the screen.
  • Level up: "+1 saved" — every level is one real animal saved. Stats shows the running total.
  • Same 3D device and look as the landing page; the page around it stays minimal.
